## App store listing

### Short description (80 chars)
> Seal agreements with evidence, OTP consent, and a tamper-proof vault.

### Full description

**Kotoku — Agreement Evidence**

When money changes hands informally, trust can break down fast. Kotoku gives both parties a record they can actually stand behind.

- **Capture** — photos of the asset, ID documents, agreed terms
- **Confirm** — both parties sign with an SMS OTP before anything is sealed
- **Seal** — a tamper-evident, timestamped vault entry is created
- **Keep** — your sealed record is retrievable whenever you need it

Good for:
- Buying or selling a used vehicle
- Renting a room or apartment
- Any deal where you want more than a verbal promise

Every sealed agreement is hashed and stored in compliance with Ghana's Electronic Transactions Act (Act 772). Your record is legally recognisable evidence.

> *"Don't take their word for it. Take evidence for it."*

### How it works (3 steps)

**Step 1 — Capture**
> Photograph the asset, upload ID documents, and fill in the agreed terms. Every piece of evidence is timestamped as it comes in.

**Step 2 — Confirm**
> Both parties receive an SMS OTP. No one can seal the agreement alone. Both confirmations are recorded.

**Step 3 — Seal**
> A tamper-evident vault entry is created — hashed, timestamped, and retrievable any time. Download the PDF or share the link.

## Tone guide

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Direct, plain English | Legal jargon |
| Second-person ("you", "your") | Passive voice |
| Specific scenarios | Vague promises |
| Warm but serious | Flippant about money disputes |
| Acknowledge informal economy | Condescend to the informal economy |
| Short sentences | Run-ons |
